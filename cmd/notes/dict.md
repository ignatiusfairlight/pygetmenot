The old lighthouse keeper had maintained his post for forty years when the storm finally came. He had weathered hurricanes,
rogue waves, and three wars, but nothing had prepared him for the silence after the light went out. He climbed the spiral 
stairs one last time, lantern in hand, knowing the ships in the harbor were counting on him. At the top, he found the bulb
shattered on the floor — and beside it, a single muddy boot print that was not his own.
